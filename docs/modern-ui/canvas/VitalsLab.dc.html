TRY IT Each button changes the bars above the way the game would.
Damage: the bar drops at once, the lost part flashes, then drains away pale; a number falls off the bar when the hit is at least 5% of it (Options). Healing: a bright edge jumps to the new value and the bar fills up behind it, with a glint. Under 25% health: the bar pulses red; the screen edge glows too (OPENAC, on by default). Not enough mana: the bar shakes and marks what the spell needs. Your own casting only drains, no number.
